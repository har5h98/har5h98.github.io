Privacy Policy

Who we are

Smallplate is run by the developer of Smallplate (“Smallplate”, “we”, “us”). Questions about privacy: our contact form.

The short version

The Smallplate app has no accounts. Your profile, meal plans, logs, shots and settings are stored on your iPhone, not on our servers. The only personal information we hold is what you give us on this website: your waitlist sign-up and anything you send through the Support form. We don't sell personal information, don't show ads, and don't use advertising or tracking SDKs.

What the app stores on your phone

What you enter: medication type and schedule (never doses we suggest), shot day, weight and goal weight, appetite, symptoms you tap, diet and allergy preferences, cuisines, household size, preferred store, an optional first name, meals you check off, water, weigh-ins, strength sessions and doses you log.

This data stays in the app's storage on your device. Like other app data, it can be included in your own iPhone backup, which Apple manages under your Apple ID. You can export it any time (Settings → Export my data) and erase it (Settings → Start over). Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Smallplate reads weight, lean body mass, body fat, dietary protein and water, GLP-1 doses you log in Health, and strength workouts. It writes protein, calories, carbs, fat and fiber from meals you check off, and water you log.

Health data is used only to run the features you see in the app. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never saved to iCloud storage by Smallplate. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Smallplate.

Notifications

Reminders (water, shot day, pill timing, the weekly check-in and your trial reminder) are scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved. If notifications are off, reminders can't reach you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your App Store purchase records and basic technical information such as device model and IP address. It does not receive your name, email, health or meal data. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, we collect your email, your answers to the optional questions, how you found us (for example the link from a video), the steps you take on the page (for example “saw the price” or “tapped the founding price”) and your device type (iPhone, Android or computer). We use it to email you about the launch and the founding price, and to understand which posts bring people here.

To connect those steps, the page keeps a random visitor ID and the link you first arrived from in your browser's local storage. We don't use cookies, advertising pixels or third-party analytics. Answers about your health are covered by our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy and are only stored if you agree.

Contact messages

If you write to us through the Support form, we store your message and the email you give us so we can reply.

Who else receives information

Google stores the waitlist and contact form (Google Forms and Google Sheets). Google states that it does not use the content of Forms and Sheets for advertising.

GitHub hosts this website and Google Fonts serves its typefaces. Like any web host, they receive your IP address and basic browser information when a page loads, under their own privacy policies.

Apple and RevenueCat handle app purchases as described above. Your email provider delivers our emails.

We would also disclose information if the law required it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of Smallplate, in which case this policy would continue to apply to it.

How long we keep it

Waitlist information: until 12 months after the app launches, or sooner if you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe. Contact messages: 12 months after we've answered. Then we delete them.

Security

We limit access to the waitlist to the people running Smallplate and protect the accounts that hold it with strong passwords and two-step verification. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affected your information, we would tell you as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop emailing you, through our contact form. Every launch email also has an unsubscribe link. We answer within 45 days and don't charge for it. If we turn a request down, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we'll review it within 45 days.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: this site doesn't track you across other sites or let anyone else do so, so it treats every visit the same whether or not your browser sends these signals. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

Children

Smallplate is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

United States only

Smallplate and this website are offered to people in the United States. Information is stored in the United States.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the date above. If a change affects information we already hold in a significant way, we'll tell you by email first. Questions: our contact form.
