Common questions

How do I cancel Pro? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Smallplate. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and the free plan stays.

Can I get a refund? Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. Install Smallplate, then Settings → Restore purchases. Your meal history moves with your iPhone backup.

Apple Health isn't syncing. Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Smallplate and check the categories are on.

Can you tell me what dose to take? No. Smallplate never gives dosing advice. Please ask your prescriber.

Delete my waitlist information. Use the form above with the email you signed up with, and we'll delete it within 45 days (usually much sooner).
