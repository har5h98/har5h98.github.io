Smoked Salmon and Dill Scramble

Soft eggs folded with ribbons of smoked salmon, dill and a spoon of cream cheese. Rich, small and fast.

No-appetite days pescatarian low carb
35 gprotein
374calories
2 gfiber
10minutes

Makes 1 serving. Per serving: 16 g carbs, 18 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg, fish, gluten.

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs and whites with a pinch of pepper.
  2. Melt the butter in a nonstick pan over low. Add the eggs and stir slowly 2-3 min until barely set.
  3. Off the heat, fold in the salmon, cream cheese and dill so the salmon just warms.
  4. Serve as is or on toast.
Small-appetite tip. Smoked salmon is salty. Have a glass of water alongside, which also helps the hydration most GLP-1 users fall behind on.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup liquid egg whites
  • 1/2 tsp butter
  • 2 oz smoked salmon, torn
  • 1 tbsp light cream cheese
  • 2 tsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 slice whole-wheat bread, toasted (optional)
