Chicken & White Bean Chili

Green chili with shredded chicken, cannellini beans and cumin, made creamy with yogurt. 8 g fiber a bowl.

Fiber + fluids gluten free
55 gprotein
453calories
8 gfiber
40minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 38 g carbs, 9 g fat. Contains: dairy.

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium; cook onion, pepper and jalapeño 5 min.
  2. Add garlic, cumin and oregano for 1 min. Mash 1 cup of the beans with a fork.
  3. Add broth, whole and mashed beans, and whole chicken breasts; cover and simmer 20 min until chicken reaches 165°F. (Or slow-cook on low 6 hr.)
  4. Lift out chicken, shred and return to the pot.
  5. Off the heat, stir in yogurt and lime juice; season with salt and top with cilantro.
Small-appetite tip. Keep servings to about 1 1/2 cups. Freeze the rest flat in bags for an easy dinner on a low-energy day.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2 (15-oz) cans cannellini beans, rinsed
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper or poblano, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
