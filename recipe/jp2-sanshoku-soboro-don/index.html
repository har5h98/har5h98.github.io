Sanshoku Soboro Don Three-Color Bowl

Bento classic: ginger-soy chicken crumble, sweet scrambled egg and green beans in stripes over rice. Good at room temp.

Easy on nausea Reflux-friendly dairy free
50 gprotein
540calories
2 gfiber
25minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 52 g carbs, 13 g fat. Contains: egg, gluten, soy.

Steps

  1. Put the chicken, soy sauce, most of the honey, ginger and water in a cold skillet. Stir with 4 chopsticks over medium heat 5-6 minutes until finely crumbled, cooked through (165°F) and the liquid is absorbed.
  2. Beat the eggs with the remaining honey and the salt. Cook in an oiled nonstick pan over low heat, stirring constantly with chopsticks, into tiny soft curds.
  3. Blanch the green beans 2 minutes in salted water and drain.
  4. Fill bowls or bento boxes with rice and lay the chicken, egg and green beans on top in three neat stripes.
Small-appetite tip. Packs well chilled and tastes fine at room temperature, which helps when warm food smells too strong. Eat the chicken stripe first.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz ground chicken breast (or finely minced breast)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tsp sugar or honey, divided
  • 2 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3/4 tsp neutral oil
  • pinch of salt
  • 4 oz green beans or snow peas, thinly sliced on the bias
  • 2 cups cooked Japanese rice
