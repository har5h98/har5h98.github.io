Vietnamese Chicken Pho with Extra Breast

Hanoi's classic breakfast: star-anise ginger broth, rice noodles, double the chicken, and a pile of fresh herbs.

gluten free dairy free
58 gprotein
491calories
4 gfiber
40minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 47 g carbs, 9 g fat. Contains: fish.

Steps

  1. Char the onion and ginger cut-side down in a dry pot over medium-high heat, 5 min, until blackened in spots.
  2. Add the coriander, cinnamon, star anise and cloves; toast 30 sec until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the broth, fish sauce and honey. Add the whole chicken breasts and simmer gently (no hard boil) for 15-18 min, until they reach 165°F.
  4. Lift out the chicken, rest 3 min, then slice thinly. Strain the broth and taste for salt.
  5. Divide the noodles between big bowls, top with chicken, and ladle over the very hot broth.
  6. Serve with sprouts, basil, cilantro, green onion, lime and chile so everyone adds their own.
Small-appetite tip. Make the broth the night before and the morning bowl takes 5 minutes. Sip broth between bites; it counts toward your fluids.

Ingredients

  • 1 small yellow onion, halved (unpeeled)
  • 3-inch piece fresh ginger, halved lengthwise
  • 1 tsp coriander seeds (or 1/2 tsp ground)
  • 1 small cinnamon stick + 2 star anise + 3 whole cloves
  • 5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 12.5 oz boneless skinless chicken breast (2 small)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp honey (stands in for rock sugar)
  • 3 oz dried flat rice noodles (bánh phở), cooked (about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • A few Thai basil sprigs
  • A handful of cilantro
  • 1 green onion, thinly sliced
  • Lime wedges, to serve
  • Sliced red chile or jalapeño, to serve (optional)
