Homemade Fish Ball Noodle Soup

Springy Teochew-style fish balls made from cod, in clear broth with rice noodles and greens. A Hong Kong breakfast.

Easy on nausea Reflux-friendly No-appetite days dairy free
53 gprotein
411calories
3 gfiber
35minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 40 g carbs, 5 g fat. Contains: egg, fish, gluten, soy.

Steps

  1. Pulse the cold fish in a food processor with the egg white, cornstarch, salt, pepper and ice water until a smooth, sticky paste. Process 1 minute more.
  2. Bring the broth and ginger to a gentle simmer, not a boil.
  3. With wet hands or two spoons, drop in 1-inch balls of paste. Simmer 5-6 minutes until they float and are firm and white through (145°F).
  4. Add the bok choy and soy sauce and simmer 1 minute.
  5. Divide the noodles between bowls, ladle in the fish balls, greens and broth, and top with green onion and cilantro.
Small-appetite tip. Fish balls are soft, bouncy and mild. On rough days skip the noodles and just sip the broth with a few balls.

Ingredients

  • 14 oz cod or other firm white fish, skinless, cubed and well chilled
  • 1/4 cup egg white
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1/3 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 2 tbsp ice water
  • 4 cups low-sodium chicken or fish stock
  • 3 thin slices ginger
  • 3 oz dried thin rice noodles, cooked (about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 2 baby bok choy or a bunch of choy sum, cut up
  • 2 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 green onion, sliced
  • a few cilantro sprigs
