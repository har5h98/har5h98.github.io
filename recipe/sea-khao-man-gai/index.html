Thai Poached Chicken & Ginger Rice

Khao man gai: silky poached chicken over broth-cooked rice with a ginger-soy sauce and a cup of clear soup to sip.

Easy on nausea dairy free
53 gprotein
476calories
1 gfiber
35minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 48 g carbs, 8 g fat. Contains: gluten, soy.

Steps

  1. Bring the broth, sliced ginger and green onion whites to a simmer. Add the chicken, cover, and keep at a bare simmer 14-16 min until it reaches 165°F.
  2. Lift out the chicken and rest 5 min. Ladle 1/2 cup broth into a small pan with the rice; cover and warm over low 4 min until the rice absorbs it.
  3. Stir the grated ginger, soy sauce, vinegar, honey and garlic (if using) into a dipping sauce.
  4. Slice the chicken and fan it over the rice with cucumber and cilantro.
  5. Season the remaining broth with green onion greens and serve a cup on the side, with the sauce for drizzling.
Small-appetite tip. Poached chicken stays tender cold, so this works as a room-temp lunch on rough days. Sip the broth first to settle in.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2-inch piece ginger: half sliced, half finely grated
  • 2 green onions: whites for poaching, greens sliced
  • 1 1/4 cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 small clove garlic, grated (optional)
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • A few cilantro sprigs
