Machaca con Huevo Breakfast Tacos

Northern Mexican shredded beef scrambled with eggs, tomato and green chile, rolled into warm flour tortillas.

dairy free
36 gprotein
416calories
3 gfiber
25minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 30 g carbs, 16 g fat. Contains: egg, gluten.

Steps

  1. Season the flank strips with salt, pepper and cumin and sear in the oil in a skillet over high heat 4 minutes until well browned.
  2. Move the beef to a board and shred it finely with two forks (machaca-style), then return it to the pan.
  3. Add the onion, green chile and garlic and cook 2 minutes; add the tomato and cook 2 minutes more.
  4. Whisk the eggs and egg whites, pour into the pan and scramble with the beef 2-3 minutes until just set.
  5. Spoon into warm tortillas and roll up.
Small-appetite tip. Shredding the beef makes it tender and easy to chew. If a full taco is too much, eat the filling first and save the tortilla.

Ingredients

  • 6 oz flank steak, cut into thin strips
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup liquid egg whites
  • 1 Roma tomato, diced
  • 1/4 white onion, diced
  • 1 roasted Anaheim chile, peeled and diced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • pinch of ground cumin
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 2 small (6-inch) flour tortillas, warmed
  • 1/3 tsp salt and pepper
