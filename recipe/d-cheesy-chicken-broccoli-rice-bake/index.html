Cheesy Chicken, Broccoli & Rice Bake

Casserole comfort made with a blended cottage-cheese sauce instead of canned soup. 50 g protein a square.

gluten free
53 gprotein
481calories
4 gfiber
45minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 37 g carbs, 13 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg.

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Steam broccoli 3 min until bright green; drain well.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, milk, egg, Dijon, garlic powder, half the cheddar and 1/2 tsp salt until smooth.
  3. Fold sauce with rice, chicken and broccoli and spread in an 8x8-in baking dish; top with remaining cheddar.
  4. Cover with foil and bake 20 min, then uncover and bake 10 min more until bubbly and 165°F in the center.
  5. Rest 5 min and cut into 4 squares.
Small-appetite tip. Blended cottage cheese makes the creamy sauce, adding protein instead of fat. Cut into 4 squares; keeps 4 days chilled.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked chicken breast, diced (rotisserie, skin removed)
  • 14 oz broccoli florets, chopped small
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1/2 cup skim milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2/3 cup shredded sharp cheddar, divided
