Air-Fryer Chicken Parm Cutlets

Crisp parmesan-crumb chicken cutlets with marinara and melty mozzarella, made in the air fryer.

low carb
56 gprotein
456calories
4 gfiber
25minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 19 g carbs, 16 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg, gluten.

Steps

  1. Heat air fryer to 400°F. Mix crumbs, parmesan and Italian seasoning on a plate.
  2. Dip each cutlet in egg white, then press into the crumb mix on both sides. Mist with oil.
  3. Air-fry 10-12 min, flipping halfway, until golden and 165°F inside.
  4. Spoon marinara over each cutlet, top with mozzarella and air-fry 2-3 min more until melted.
  5. Serve over arugula with torn basil.
Small-appetite tip. Thin cutlets mean one is a full serving with plenty of protein. Skip the pasta side; arugula adds crunch without filling you too fast.

Ingredients

  • 2 small chicken breasts (12 oz), halved horizontally into cutlets
  • 1/4 cup liquid egg whites
  • 1 slice whole-wheat bread, toasted and blitzed into crumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 3/4 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp olive oil (or oil spray)
  • 2/3 cup no-sugar-added marinara, warmed
  • 1/2 cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
  • 2 cups arugula, to serve
  • A few basil leaves (optional)
