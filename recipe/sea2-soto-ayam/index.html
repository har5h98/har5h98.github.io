Soto Ayam Turmeric Chicken Soup

Indonesia's golden chicken soup with lemongrass and turmeric, glass noodles, sprouts and egg. Fragrant, not spicy.

No-appetite days gluten free dairy free
55 gprotein
462calories
3 gfiber
45minutes

Makes 3 servings. Per serving: 31 g carbs, 14 g fat. Contains: egg.

Steps

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, ginger, turmeric and coriander with 2 tbsp water into a paste.
  2. Fry the paste in the oil in a large pot over medium 4 minutes until fragrant and deep yellow.
  3. Add the lemongrass, broth and chicken. Simmer covered 20 minutes until the chicken is 165°F.
  4. Lift out the chicken, shred it, and discard the lemongrass.
  5. Divide the noodles, sprouts, cabbage, chicken and eggs among bowls, ladle over the hot broth and finish with herbs and a squeeze of lime. Add sambal at the table only if you want heat.
Small-appetite tip. Keep the broth and toppings separate in the fridge and build a fresh bowl each time. A cup of broth with egg is a light meal on its own.

Ingredients

  • 3 shallots, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1-inch piece ginger
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric (or 1-inch fresh)
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 1/2 tsp neutral oil
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, bruised and knotted
  • 5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 lb 2 oz chicken breast
  • 1 1/2 oz glass noodles (soun), soaked
  • 5 oz bean sprouts, blanched
  • 1 cup finely shredded cabbage
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, halved
  • lime wedges, to serve
  • celery leaves or cilantro, to serve
