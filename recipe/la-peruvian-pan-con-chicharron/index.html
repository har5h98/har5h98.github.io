Lighter Peruvian Pan con Chicharrón

Lima's Sunday sandwich with seared pork tenderloin, roasted sweet potato and tangy salsa criolla. 37 g protein.

dairy free
37 gprotein
423calories
4 gfiber
30minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 47 g carbs, 10 g fat. Contains: gluten.

Steps

  1. Heat the oven or air fryer to 425°F. Toss the sweet potato with 1 tsp oil and a pinch of salt; roast 16-18 min, flipping once.
  2. Rub the pork with the garlic, cumin, salt and pepper.
  3. For the salsa criolla, soak the onion in cold water 5 min, drain, then toss with lime juice, chile, cilantro and a pinch of salt.
  4. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear the pork 2-3 min per side until browned and 145°F inside.
  5. Split and warm the rolls. Layer sweet potato, pork and a pile of salsa criolla.
Small-appetite tip. If a whole roll is too much, eat it open-faced on half the bread. The pork and sweet potato are the parts to finish.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz pork tenderloin, sliced 1/4 in thick
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 small sweet potato, sliced in 1/4-in rounds
  • 2 small crusty white rolls (pan francés), about 1.75 oz each
  • 1/2 red onion, slivered
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/2 jalapeño or ají amarillo, slivered (optional)
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 2 tsp oil, divided
  • 1/4 tsp salt, plus pepper
