Pork Tonnato with Arugula

Piedmont's vitello tonnato made with lean pork tenderloin: cold slices under a tuna-caper sauce. 46 g protein.

low carb
46 gprotein
353calories
1 gfiber
40minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 20 g carbs, 9 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg, fish, gluten.

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Rub the pork with the oil, salt and pepper and roast 20-25 min until 145°F inside. Rest 10 min, then chill.
  2. Blend the tuna, yogurt, mayo, capers and lemon juice until smooth; thin with water to a pourable sauce.
  3. Slice the cold pork very thinly and fan it over the arugula.
  4. Spoon the tuna sauce over, scatter a few capers and serve with bread.
Small-appetite tip. Pack pork, sauce and greens separately; they keep 3 days. Cold sliced meat is easy to eat a few bites at a time.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/3 lb pork tenderloin (veal is traditional)
  • 1 (5-oz) can tuna, drained
  • 2/3 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 3 tbsp light mayo
  • 2 tbsp capers, plus more to serve
  • 1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 4 cups arugula
  • 4 slices crusty bread, to serve
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus pepper
