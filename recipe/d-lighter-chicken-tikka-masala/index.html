Lighter Chicken Tikka Masala

Broiler-charred yogurt chicken in a spiced tomato sauce finished with Greek yogurt instead of cream.

gluten free
48 gprotein
414calories
6 gfiber
35minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 33 g carbs, 10 g fat. Contains: dairy.

Steps

  1. Toss chicken with 1/3 cup yogurt, half the curry powder, the turmeric and 1/2 tsp salt.
  2. Spread on a foil-lined pan and broil on high 8-10 min, turning once, until charred at the edges and 165°F inside.
  3. Meanwhile heat oil in a skillet over medium; cook onion 6 min, then garlic, ginger and remaining curry powder 1 min.
  4. Stir in tomato paste for 1 min, add tomatoes and simmer 10 min; blend until smooth.
  5. Off the heat, stir in the remaining yogurt, then add chicken and warm 2 min over low. Serve over rice with cilantro.
Small-appetite tip. Greek yogurt replaces cream, adding protein instead of fat. The sauce tastes even better on day two, so make it ahead.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut in 1 1/2-in chunks
  • 3/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, divided
  • 1 tbsp garam masala or curry powder, divided
  • 3/4 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 (14.5-oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked brown basmati rice
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
