Coronation Chicken Lettuce Cups

The British picnic classic, remade with Greek yogurt: mildly curried chicken with apricot and celery in crisp lettuce.

gluten free low carb
48 gprotein
332calories
4 gfiber
15minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 17 g carbs, 8 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg.

Steps

  1. Toast the curry powder in a dry skillet over low heat for 30 seconds until fragrant, then tip into a bowl.
  2. Stir in the yogurt, mayo, lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  3. Fold in the chicken, apricots and celery.
  4. Spoon into the lettuce leaves and scatter with parsley.
Small-appetite tip. Keeps 3 days in the fridge. Try two cups first and wait 10 minutes before reaching for the rest.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups diced cooked chicken breast (about 9 oz)
  • 1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 1/2 tbsp light mayonnaise
  • 1 1/2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 4 dried apricots, finely chopped (in place of mango chutney)
  • 1 celery rib, finely diced
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 8 little gem or romaine heart leaves
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley or cilantro
