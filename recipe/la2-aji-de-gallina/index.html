Lighter Ají de Gallina

Peru's creamy shredded chicken in golden ají amarillo sauce, made with skim milk. Soft, rich and 51 g protein.

No-appetite days
51 gprotein
487calories
2 gfiber
40minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 33 g carbs, 16 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg, gluten, nuts.

Steps

  1. Poach the chicken in the broth plus water to cover, 15-18 min until 165°F. Shred finely and keep 1/2 cup of the liquid.
  2. Soak the bread in the milk 5 min, then blend with the walnuts and Parmesan until smooth.
  3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium. Cook the onion 6 min until soft, then the garlic, ají and turmeric 2 min.
  4. Pour in the milk mixture and simmer, stirring, 4 min until it coats a spoon. Add the chicken, salt and enough reserved liquid to make it creamy.
  5. Serve over a small scoop of rice with egg and olives.
Small-appetite tip. Soft and dense, a 3/4-cup scoop is a full meal. Leftovers keep 3 days; loosen with a splash of milk when reheating.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp ají amarillo paste (or 2 seeded jalapeños + 1/2 yellow bell pepper)
  • 1 slice bread, crusts removed
  • 1 1/2 cups skim milk (in place of evaporated milk)
  • 2 tbsp walnuts or pecans
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, quartered
  • 4 black botija or kalamata olives
  • 1 1/3 cups cooked white rice, to serve
  • 3/4 tsp salt
