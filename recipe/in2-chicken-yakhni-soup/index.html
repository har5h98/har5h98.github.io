Pakistani Chicken Yakhni Soup

A clear, peppery broth simmered with whole spices and plenty of shredded chicken. Sip it slowly from a mug.

Easy on nausea No-appetite days gluten free dairy free low carb
43 gprotein
329calories
1 gfiber
40minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 18 g carbs, 8 g fat. Contains: none of the top allergens.

Steps

  1. Put the stock, 1 cup water, ginger, garlic, onion, whole spices and peppercorns in a pot and bring to a simmer.
  2. Add the chicken and simmer gently, covered, 18-20 min until 165°F inside.
  3. Lift out the chicken and shred it. Strain the broth and discard the spices.
  4. Return the chicken to the broth, season with the salt and warm through 2 min.
  5. Ladle into mugs and finish with cilantro.
Small-appetite tip. Sip the broth between bites of chicken; small sips are easier than a full bowl. Freeze portions in mugs for shot day.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 1-inch piece ginger, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 1/2 small onion, halved
  • 1 small cinnamon stick, plus 4 cloves and 3 green cardamom pods
  • 1/2 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
