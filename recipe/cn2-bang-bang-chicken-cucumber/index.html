Bang Bang Chicken with Cucumber & Noodles

Sichuan cold shredded chicken over cucumber and glass noodles, in a nutty sesame, black vinegar and chili oil sauce.

dairy free
51 gprotein
504calories
3 gfiber
30minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 36 g carbs, 17 g fat. Contains: gluten, peanut, sesame, soy.

Steps

  1. Poach the chicken with the ginger and 1 green onion in barely simmering water 12-14 minutes until 165°F. Cool, then shred by hand.
  2. Soak the glass noodles in hot water 8 minutes, drain, rinse cold and cut into shorter lengths.
  3. Whisk the sesame paste, soy sauce, vinegar, honey, garlic, sesame or chili oil and chili flakes with 1-2 tbsp water until pourable.
  4. Layer the noodles and cucumber on plates, pile the chicken on top and pour over the sauce.
  5. Finish with peanuts and the sliced green onion.
Small-appetite tip. Keep sauce and chicken separate for meal prep and dress just before eating. Go light on chili oil if heat sits badly with you.

Ingredients

  • 14 oz chicken breast
  • 3 slices ginger, for poaching
  • 2 green onions: 1 for poaching, 1 sliced
  • 2 oz mung bean glass noodles
  • 2 Persian cucumbers, julienned
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Chinese sesame paste or tahini
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Chinese black vinegar or rice vinegar
  • 1 1/2 tsp sugar or honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • 1 tsp sesame oil or chili oil
  • 1/2 tsp Sichuan chili flakes, plus ground Sichuan pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp roasted peanuts, crushed
