Lebanese Chicken & Spiced Rice

Riz a djej: tender poached chicken over cinnamon-scented rice with peas and toasted almonds. Mild and comforting.

Easy on nausea Reflux-friendly gluten free
53 gprotein
507calories
3 gfiber
40minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 46 g carbs, 11 g fat. Contains: dairy, nuts.

Steps

  1. Simmer the broth with the cinnamon stick in a large pot. Add the chicken, cover and poach on low 18 min until 165°F inside. Lift out, shred and keep warm.
  2. Measure 1 3/4 cups of the poaching broth (top up with water if needed).
  3. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium, add the rice, ground cinnamon and coriander and stir 1 min until the grains turn opaque.
  4. Pour in the measured broth and salt, bring to a boil, cover and cook on low 15 min. Scatter the peas on top, cover and rest 5 min off the heat.
  5. Fluff the rice onto a platter, pile on the chicken, and finish with almonds and parsley. Serve with yogurt on the side.
Small-appetite tip. No onion, garlic or tomato here, so it's a safe pick on reflux nights. Keep the extra broth in a mug and sip it alongside.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 cinnamon stick plus 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1 cup long-grain rice, rinsed (cooks to about 3 cups)
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp slivered almonds, toasted
  • 3/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, to serve
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
