Hungarian Chicken Paprikash

Chicken simmered in sweet paprika, onion and pepper, finished with tangy yogurt and sour cream over a few noodles.

50 gprotein
459calories
4 gfiber
40minutes

Makes 3 servings. Per serving: 30 g carbs, 15 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg, gluten.

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium and cook the onions 8 min until soft and golden.
  2. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the paprika so it doesn't scorch.
  3. Add the chicken, pepper, tomato, broth and salt. Cover and simmer 25 min until the chicken is tender and 165°F.
  4. Whisk the yogurt and sour cream with a ladle of the hot sauce, then stir it into the pot off the heat.
  5. Serve over a small portion of noodles.
Small-appetite tip. Spoon the chicken and sauce first and treat the noodles as a side. It freezes well before the yogurt goes in.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, in large pieces
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 1 Hungarian wax or red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped
  • 1 1/2 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika
  • 2 tsp oil
  • 1 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/3 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp light sour cream
  • 1 cup cooked egg noodles (nokedli if you have them)
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
