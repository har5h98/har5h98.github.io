Cantonese Silky Egg & Shrimp

Waat daan haa yan: shrimp folded into barely set, silky scrambled eggs with scallions. A 15-minute home classic.

pescatarian gluten free dairy free
57 gprotein
548calories
2 gfiber
15minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 41 g carbs, 17 g fat. Contains: egg, sesame, shellfish.

Steps

  1. Toss the shrimp with the cornstarch and half the salt. Beat the eggs, whites, remaining salt, pepper, sesame oil and most of the green onion.
  2. Heat half the oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high and cook the shrimp 1 1/2 minutes per side until pink. Tip them into the eggs.
  3. Wipe the pan, add the remaining oil and turn the heat to low.
  4. Pour in the egg mixture and gently push it from the edges to the center every few seconds, 2-3 minutes, until just set and still glossy.
  5. Slide onto plates, scatter the remaining green onion and serve with bok choy and rice.
Small-appetite tip. Soft eggs and shrimp are easy to eat in small bites. Take it off the heat while it still looks slightly wet; it finishes on the plate.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz peeled shrimp, patted dry
  • 1/2 tsp cornstarch
  • 1/3 tsp salt, divided, plus white pepper
  • 5 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup + 1 tbsp liquid egg whites
  • 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp neutral oil, divided
  • 2 green onions, finely sliced
  • 3 baby bok choy, blanched
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked jasmine rice
