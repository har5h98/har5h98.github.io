British Kedgeree with Cod & Eggs

The classic Victorian breakfast: flaked fish, gently curried rice, peas and soft-boiled egg. Protein-heavy, not spicy.

pescatarian gluten free
42 gprotein
436calories
6 gfiber
30minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 41 g carbs, 11 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg, fish.

Steps

  1. Lower the eggs into simmering water and cook 7 minutes, then cool in cold water, peel and quarter.
  2. Lay the fish in a skillet, cover with water and simmer gently 5-6 minutes until it flakes. Lift out, remove skin and break into large flakes.
  3. Wipe the pan, melt the butter over medium and soften the onion for 5 minutes. Stir in the curry powder for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the rice, peas and 3 tbsp water. Toss 3-4 minutes until hot and the peas are tender.
  5. Off the heat, fold in the fish, yogurt, lemon juice and most of the parsley. Top with the eggs and remaining parsley.
Small-appetite tip. Over 40 g protein per bowl. Eat the fish and egg first and leave the rice; leftovers reheat well for lunch.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz smoked haddock or cod fillet
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked brown or basmati rice
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup frozen peas
  • 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp butter
  • 1 1/2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 3 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 2 tsp lemon juice, plus wedges
