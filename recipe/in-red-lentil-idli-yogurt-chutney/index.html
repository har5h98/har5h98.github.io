Steamed Red Lentil Idli with Coconut Dahi

Soft steamed lentil-yogurt cakes with ginger and carrot, plus a mild coconut-yogurt chutney. No onion, garlic or chile.

Easy on nausea Reflux-friendly vegetarian pescatarian gluten free
34 gprotein
427calories
9 gfiber
55minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 57 g carbs, 8 g fat. Contains: dairy, nuts.

Steps

  1. Soak the lentils in hot water for at least 30 min (or overnight in the fridge). Drain well.
  2. Blend the lentils with half the yogurt and 1/4 cup water to a thick, smooth batter. Stir in the carrot, half the ginger, half the cilantro and most of the salt.
  3. Heat the oil in a small pan, add 1/2 tsp mustard seeds and a few curry leaves if you have them, and once they pop pour over the batter. Stir in 1/4 tsp baking soda.
  4. Spoon into greased idli molds or silicone muffin cups. Steam in a covered pot over simmering water 12-14 min until a toothpick comes out clean.
  5. Stir the remaining yogurt with the coconut milk, remaining ginger, cilantro and a pinch of salt. Serve 6 idli each with the chutney.
Small-appetite tip. Idli are soft, mild and fine at room temp, a good first food on a queasy morning. Freeze extras and re-steam 3 min.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup dry red lentils (masoor dal), rinsed
  • 1 1/4 cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt, divided
  • 1 small carrot, finely grated
  • 2 tsp grated fresh ginger, divided
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro, divided
  • 1 tsp avocado oil, plus a little to grease
  • 2 tbsp canned coconut milk
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt, divided
