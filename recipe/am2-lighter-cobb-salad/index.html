Lighter Cobb Salad

The Hollywood classic in tidy rows: chicken, egg, avocado, tomato, turkey bacon and blue cheese. 48 g protein.

gluten free low carb
48 gprotein
459calories
6 gfiber
25minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 11 g carbs, 24 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg.

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and cook in a skillet over medium 6 min per side until 165°F. Rest 5 min and dice.
  2. Whisk the vinegar, Dijon and oil.
  3. Spread the romaine on 2 plates and arrange the chicken, egg, tomatoes, avocado, turkey bacon and cheese in rows. Add the green onion.
  4. Drizzle with the dressing just before eating.
Small-appetite tip. Eat across the rows starting with chicken and egg. Packed with dressing on the side, it keeps 2 days.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 1 head romaine, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 avocado, diced
  • 2 slices turkey bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled
  • 1 oz crumbled blue cheese
  • 1 green onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp salt, plus pepper
