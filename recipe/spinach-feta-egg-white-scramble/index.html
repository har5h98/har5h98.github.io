Spinach-Feta Egg White Scramble

Fluffy whites with one whole egg, wilted spinach and salty feta on sourdough. Big protein, light on the stomach.

vegetarian pescatarian
42 gprotein
425calories
3 gfiber
12minutes

Makes 1 serving. Per serving: 29 g carbs, 15 g fat. Contains: dairy, egg, gluten.

Steps

  1. Whisk the egg whites and whole egg with a pinch of pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a nonstick skillet over medium. Add the spinach and tomatoes and cook 1-2 min until the spinach wilts.
  3. Lower the heat to medium-low, pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula for 2-3 min until just set in soft curds.
  4. Fold in the feta off the heat and spoon over the toast.
Small-appetite tip. Eat the eggs first and the toast last. If you fill up halfway, the protein is already in.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup liquid egg whites
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 2 packed cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3/4 oz feta, crumbled
  • 1 slice sourdough, toasted
  • Pinch of black pepper
