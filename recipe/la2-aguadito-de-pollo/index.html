Peruvian Aguadito de Pollo

Lima's cilantro-green chicken and rice soup with peas, carrot and corn. A big pot that feeds the week.

gluten free dairy free
52 gprotein
466calories
5 gfiber
45minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 43 g carbs, 10 g fat. Contains: none of the top allergens.

Steps

  1. Blend the cilantro and spinach with 1/2 cup of the broth until very smooth.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium. Cook the onion 4 min, then the garlic, ají and cumin 1 min.
  3. Add the remaining broth, the chicken and the salt. Simmer covered 15 min until the chicken is 165°F; lift out and shred.
  4. Add the rice, carrot and bell pepper and simmer 15 min until the rice is soft.
  5. Stir in the cilantro purée, peas, corn and chicken and simmer 5 min. Serve with lime.
Small-appetite tip. Warm broth helps hydration on low-intake days. Freeze in single portions; the rice keeps thickening, so add a splash of water.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2 bunches cilantro, leaves and tender stems
  • 2 handfuls spinach (for color)
  • 1/2 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp ají amarillo paste (or 1 seeded jalapeño)
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup raw long-grain white rice (cooks to about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 large carrot, diced
  • 3/4 cup corn kernels
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • lime wedges, to serve
  • 3/4 tsp salt
