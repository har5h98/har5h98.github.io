Persian Halim: Chicken & Wheat Porridge

Iran's winter breakfast: shredded chicken beaten into soft wheat with cinnamon. Mild, warm and very high in protein.

Easy on nausea Reflux-friendly No-appetite days
35 gprotein
326calories
7 gfiber
35minutes

Makes 3 servings. Per serving: 34 g carbs, 6 g fat. Contains: dairy, gluten.

Steps

  1. Bring the broth and cinnamon stick to a simmer in a medium pot. Add the chicken, cover and poach on low 15-18 min until 165°F inside.
  2. Lift out the chicken, discard the cinnamon stick and shred the meat very finely with 2 forks.
  3. Add the cooked bulgur and chicken to the broth. Simmer 10 min, stirring often, until thick.
  4. Beat hard with a wooden spoon or pulse 5-6 times with an immersion blender until stretchy and porridge-like. Season with salt.
  5. Serve in bowls topped with a drizzle of butter, a dusting of cinnamon and a little honey.
Small-appetite tip. Freeze in 1-cup portions. A small warm bowl is gentle when food feels like a chore, and the chicken is already hidden in every spoonful.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 1/2 cups cooked coarse bulgur or cracked wheat (from about 1 cup dry)
  • 1 cinnamon stick plus 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon to serve
  • 1 tsp butter, melted, to drizzle
  • 1/2 tbsp honey or sugar, to taste
  • 1/3 tsp kosher salt
