Bulgogi Jeongol Beef & Mushroom Hot Pot

Sweet-savory bulgogi beef simmered at the table with mushrooms, napa and tofu in a light soy broth.

dairy free low carb
52 gprotein
406calories
4 gfiber
35minutes

Makes 3 servings. Per serving: 21 g carbs, 14 g fat. Contains: gluten, sesame, soy.

Steps

  1. Toss the beef with the soy sauce, grated pear, garlic, honey and sesame oil and marinate 15 minutes.
  2. Arrange the napa, mushrooms, tofu and carrot in sections around a wide shallow pot, leaving the center open.
  3. Pile the marinated beef in the center and pour the broth around the edges.
  4. Bring to a boil, then simmer 8-10 minutes, stirring the beef apart, until it is cooked through and the vegetables are tender.
  5. Scatter green onion on top and ladle beef, vegetables and broth into bowls at the table.
Small-appetite tip. A brothy hot pot lets you eat slowly and stop when full. Ladle extra broth into your bowl for fluids.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb top sirloin, very thinly sliced
  • 2 1/2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/3 Asian or Bosc pear, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, grated
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 8 shiitake mushrooms, stems removed
  • 5 oz enoki or button mushrooms
  • 4 cups napa cabbage, cut in 2-inch pieces
  • 5 oz firm tofu, sliced
  • 1 small carrot, sliced on the bias
  • 3 green onions, cut in lengths
