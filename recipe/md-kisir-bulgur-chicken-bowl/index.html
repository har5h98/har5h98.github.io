Turkish Kısır Bulgur Bowl with Chicken

Bright Turkish bulgur salad with parsley, mint, tomato and pomegranate, topped with cumin-grilled chicken.

dairy free
46 gprotein
444calories
10 gfiber
25minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 42 g carbs, 12 g fat. Contains: gluten.

Steps

  1. Pound the chicken to an even 3/4 inch, rub with the cumin, salt and 1 tsp oil. Grill or pan-sear over medium-high 5-6 minutes per side until 165°F. Rest, then slice.
  2. While it cooks, stir the tomato paste into the warm bulgur with the remaining oil and lemon juice until evenly red.
  3. Fold in the parsley, mint, green onions, cucumber and tomato. Season with salt, pepper and a pinch of chili flakes if you like.
  4. Divide into bowls, top with the chicken and scatter with pomegranate seeds.
Small-appetite tip. Kısır keeps 3 days and tastes better on day two. Pack the chicken on top and eat it first at lunch.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 cups cooked fine bulgur (about 3/4 cup dry)
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste (or Turkish pepper paste)
  • 1 cup chopped parsley
  • 2 tbsp chopped mint
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 1/2 cucumber, finely diced
  • 1 large tomato, finely diced
  • 1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp pomegranate seeds
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
