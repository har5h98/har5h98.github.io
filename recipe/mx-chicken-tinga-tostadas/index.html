Chicken Tinga Tostadas

Smoky chipotle-tomato shredded chicken piled on crisp tostadas with refried pintos, lettuce and crema.

gluten free
50 gprotein
480calories
10 gfiber
30minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 44 g carbs, 12 g fat. Contains: dairy.

Steps

  1. Simmer the chicken in salted water, covered, 15-18 minutes until 165°F; shred and save 1/2 cup of the broth.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, garlic, chipotle and oregano until smooth.
  3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and cook the onion 5 minutes until soft; add the sauce and simmer 3 minutes.
  4. Stir in the chicken and reserved broth and simmer 5 minutes until saucy, not wet.
  5. Bake the tortillas on a rack at 400°F for 8-10 minutes until crisp, then spread with warm mashed pintos and top with tinga, lettuce, crema and queso fresco.
Small-appetite tip. Tinga keeps 4 days and freezes well. If a tostada is too much crunch, spoon the tinga over a little lettuce and beans instead.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 3/4 cup canned diced tomatoes
  • 1/2 white onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 chipotle in adobo (or 1 tsp smoked paprika + pinch cayenne)
  • 1/2 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 4 corn tortillas, baked into tostadas
  • 1/2 cup canned pinto beans, mashed
  • 1 cup shredded romaine
  • 3 tbsp nonfat Greek yogurt, as crema
  • 2/3 oz queso fresco, crumbled
