Lamb Arayes with Cucumber Yogurt

Lebanese pitas stuffed with spiced lamb and lean beef, griddled until crisp. Served with a cool yogurt-cucumber salad.

49 gprotein
580calories
5 gfiber
25minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 44 g carbs, 24 g fat. Contains: dairy, gluten.

Steps

  1. Mix the beef, lamb, onion, parsley, garlic, cumin, cinnamon and half the salt with your hands until tacky. Add a pinch of allspice if you have it.
  2. Spread a quarter of the meat in a thin, even layer inside each pita pocket, right to the edges. Press flat and brush the outsides with oil.
  3. Cook in a large skillet or grill pan over medium 5-6 min per side, pressing with a spatula, until crisp and the meat reaches 160°F.
  4. Meanwhile, stir the yogurt, cucumber, mint and remaining salt together.
  5. Cut each pocket into 2 triangles and serve with the cucumber yogurt for dipping.
Small-appetite tip. Arayes are rich, so 2 triangles plus the yogurt may be plenty. Wrap the rest; they re-crisp in a 375°F air fryer in 5 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz extra-lean (95%) ground beef
  • 4 oz ground lamb
  • 1/2 small onion, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1/4 cup chopped parsley
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • 3/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 whole-wheat pitas, halved into pockets
  • 1 tsp olive oil, for brushing
  • 2/3 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup diced cucumber
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh mint
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
