Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki

Lemon-oregano chicken skewers with cool tzatziki, tomato-cucumber salad and half a pita.

49 gprotein
400calories
4 gfiber
30minutes

Makes 4 servings. Per serving: 27 g carbs, 11 g fat. Contains: dairy, gluten.

Steps

  1. Whisk half the lemon juice with the oil, two-thirds of the garlic, oregano and 1/2 tsp salt; toss with chicken and marinate 10 min.
  2. For tzatziki, squeeze the grated cucumber dry and mix with yogurt, dill, remaining garlic and a pinch of salt.
  3. Thread chicken onto skewers. Grill or broil on high 10-12 min, turning, until 165°F inside.
  4. Toss diced cucumber, tomatoes, onion, remaining lemon juice and feta.
  5. Serve skewers with salad, tzatziki and half a warm pita each.
Small-appetite tip. Tzatziki adds protein and coolness. Fill half the plate with salad and use the pita to scoop rather than stuff.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut in 1-in cubes
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 3/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 English cucumber (half grated, half diced)
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill
  • 1 1/2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 whole-wheat pitas, halved
  • 1 oz feta, crumbled (optional)
