Dak-naengchae Chilled Chicken Salad

Cool shredded chicken, cucumber, napa and pear in a sharp Korean mustard dressing. Crunchy, bright and no-cook.

dairy free low carb
49 gprotein
380calories
4 gfiber
15minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 20 g carbs, 11 g fat. Contains: egg, gluten, sesame, soy.

Steps

  1. Whisk the mustard, rice vinegar, soy sauce, honey and garlic into a dressing; it should be sharp and nose-tingling.
  2. Arrange the cucumber, napa, carrot and pear in piles on two plates with the shredded chicken in the center.
  3. Add the egg halves, drizzle with dressing and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  4. Toss everything together at the table just before eating.
Small-appetite tip. Keep the dressing separate if packing it. Eat the chicken and egg first; the crunchy vegetables can wait if you fill up.

Ingredients

  • 9 oz cooked chicken breast, shredded (poached or rotisserie, skin removed)
  • 1/2 English cucumber, julienned
  • 2 cups thinly sliced napa cabbage
  • 1 small carrot, julienned
  • 1/2 Asian or Bosc pear, julienned
  • 2 large eggs, hard-boiled and halved
  • 1 tbsp Korean hot mustard (gyeoja) or yellow mustard
  • 4 tsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 small clove garlic, grated
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
