Air-Fryer Chicken Katsu with Cabbage

Crisp panko chicken cutlets made in the air fryer with a quick katsu sauce and a pile of shredded cabbage.

dairy free
56 gprotein
558calories
4 gfiber
25minutes

Makes 2 servings. Per serving: 59 g carbs, 10 g fat. Contains: egg, gluten, soy.

Steps

  1. Season the chicken, dip in egg white, then press firmly into the panko on both sides.
  2. Mist both sides with oil and air fry at 400°F for 12-14 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and 165°F inside.
  3. Stir the tomato paste, soy sauce, honey, mustard and 1 tbsp water into a quick katsu sauce.
  4. Soak the shredded cabbage in ice water 5 minutes for crunch, then drain well.
  5. Slice the katsu into strips and serve with the cabbage, rice and sauce.
Small-appetite tip. Slice the cutlet into strips and eat a few with cabbage first. Leftover katsu reheats crisp in the air fryer in 4 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 14 oz chicken breast, butterflied and pounded 1/2 inch thick
  • pinch of salt and pepper
  • 1/4 cup egg whites, beaten
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 1/2 tsp oil, for spraying
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 1/2 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 3 cups very finely shredded green cabbage
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked rice
