Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Why this page exists

Some US states, including Washington (My Health My Data Act), Nevada and Connecticut, give extra protection to “consumer health data”. Because Smallplate is about life on a GLP-1 medication, we treat everything below as consumer health data, collect it only with your consent or when it's needed for something you asked for, and never sell it. Smallplate is run by the developer of Smallplate (“Smallplate”, “we”, “us”).

What we collect

Waitlist questions (optional): where you are with your GLP-1 (for example “first 3 months” or “lowering or stopping”) and what's hardest right now (for example nausea or constipation). We store them only if you tap Continue after reading the consent note; Skip stores no answers.

Your waitlist email: signing up on a GLP-1 meal-planning site may suggest you take or are considering a GLP-1, so we treat the email and how you found us as consumer health data too. We collect it because you asked to hear about the launch.

Support messages: anything you choose to tell us about your health when you write to us.

The app: the health information you enter in the app (weight, shots, symptoms, meals) stays on your iPhone. It isn't sent to us, so we don't collect it. See the Privacy Policy.

Where it comes from and how we use it

Only from you, directly. We use it to send launch emails that fit where you are, to set up your first week when the app launches, and to decide what to build. We don't use it for advertising, don't build ad audiences from it, and don't combine it with data from other sources.

Who receives it

Google, which stores our waitlist form and spreadsheet (Google Forms and Google Sheets). We don't share consumer health data with anyone else, except if the law requires it. We never sell it.

Your rights

You can find out whether we hold your consumer health data and see it, see the list of everyone it was shared with, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent, at any time, through our contact form. Withdrawing consent means we delete your answers; you can stay on the waitlist without them. We respond within 45 days (we may extend once by 45 days if needed and will tell you why). If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; we'll decide within 45 days and, if we deny the appeal, tell you how to contact your state attorney general.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects data we already hold, we'll ask for your consent again before using it the new way. Last updated November 12, 2026.
