Why it's different

Built for what actually goes wrong on a GLP‑1.

Protein you can actually eat.

Most meals get about a third of their calories from protein, which nutrition experts say to prioritize on a GLP‑1, with strength training, to help keep muscle.1 Can't face food? Switch to a minimum day of small bites and sips.

Easy on your stomach.

Dose days and the week after a dose increase plan themselves: small, mild meals. If something didn't sit well, tap it and it's gone for good.

No calorie counting.

Check off the plate you ate and protein, water and fiber fill in. Off-plan meal? Add it with one number. Hide the scale if you'd rather not see it.

Ready for what comes next.

Lowering your dose or stopping? Your plan shifts to keeping it off, with a plan for when food noise comes back and a heads-up if the trend creeps up.

Which GLP‑1s is it for?

Any of them: semaglutide or tirzepatide, brand-name or compounded, weekly shot or daily pill. It works if you're lowering your dose or stopping, too.

No. Smallplate plans meals and never tells you how much medication to take. That stays with your prescriber.
